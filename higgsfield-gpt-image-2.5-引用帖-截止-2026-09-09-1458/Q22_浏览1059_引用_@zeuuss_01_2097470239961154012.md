## 正文

GPT-Image 2.5 不是画质升级。它是一套权限系统。

你不再向模型要一张新图。你开始告诉它：旧图里哪一部分允许动。

我文件里常备的 13 条，原样复制：

1. 锁画面：这张是母版，每个可见属性除非点名否则锁死，只改 [ELEMENT]，其余像素级返回。  
2. 一次一个变量：给 N 版，机位光调色布局相同，只变 [VARIABLE]。  
3. 风格圣经：图1 只取调色、光方向、材质完成度，套到新主体，不抄构图和主体。  
4. 同一角色新姿势：规范角色，新姿势，服装比例脸颜色相同，只改姿势和机位。  
5. 三视图：正/侧/背全身，剪影比例配件表面细节精确，正交无透视。  
6. 日变夜：地点机位几何物体相同，只改时刻，阴影反射一致重打光。  
7. 可平铺纹理：标记区域做成无缝贴图，颗粒颜色比例保留，无接缝无烘焙光。  
8. 能活下来的字：精确渲染这段字，拼写间距正确一行，贴在指定表面并跟透视材质。  
9. 去掉一件：删物体，只重建它盖住的区域。  
10. 三个焦距：主体位置光相同，24/50/85mm。  
11. 把我的图印上去：图1 不准重画，贴到物体上顺着褶皱曲面材质。  
12. 截图进场景：界面锁死当完成稿，放进桌上设备，真实屏幕辉光反射景深。  
13. 草图变参考：每个元素标签关系位置不变，只改间距对齐线宽对比。

十三条同一模式：先点名什么留下，再点名什么改，而且要无聊地明确。每个你没定义的词，模型都会替你决定。到 Higgsfield 跑这些，看有多少其实没动。

## 原文

GPT-Image 2.5 is not a quality upgrade. it's a permissions system..

you stop asking a model for a new image.

you start telling it which part of the old one is allowed to move..

13 prompts I keep in a file, copy them as they are:

1) lock the frame

this image is the master. every visible attribute is locked unless I name it. change only [ELEMENT]. return the rest pixel for pixel.

2) one variable at a time

give me [N] versions of this. camera, lighting, palette and layout stay identical. the only thing that changes is [VARIABLE]

3) the style bible

treat image 1 as the style reference. take from it only the palette, the light direction and the material finish. apply that to [NEW SUBJECT]. do not copy its composition or its subject.

4) same character, new pose

image 1 is the canonical character. generate the same character in [POSE], same wardrobe, same proportions, same face, same colours.
change only pose and camera angle. flat neutral light.

5) turnaround sheet

using image 1 as the only reference, produce a [FRONT/SIDE/BACK] full body view. keep silhouette, proportions, accessories and surface detail exact. orthographic, no perspective, plain background.

6) day into night

same location, same camera, same geometry, same objects. change only the time of day to [TIME]. relight everything consistently, including shadows and reflections. move nothing.

7) tileable texture

take the surface in the marked region and turn it into a seamless tileable texture. preserve grain, colour and scale. no seams, no lighting baked in, no objects.

8) text that survives

render exactly this text on the image: "[EXACT STRING]". correct spelling, correct spacing, one line. place it on [SURFACE] and let it follow that surface's perspective and material. add no other text.

9) remove one thing

remove [OBJECT] from the scene. rebuild only the area it covered, using the surrounding surfaces. do not repaint, relight or reframe anything else.

10) three focal lengths

same subject, same position, same light. give me the shot at 24mm, 50mm and 85mm. change nothing but the lens and the resulting compression.

11) put my artwork on it

image 1 is my artwork and must not be redrawn. apply it to [OBJECT] so it follows the real folds, curvature and material of the surface. keep every line and colour of the original.

12) screenshot into a scene

the uploaded interface is locked artwork. do not redesign it, do not change a single label. place it inside a [DEVICE] on a desk with realistic screen glow, reflections and depth of field.

13) sketch into reference

rebuild this rough sketch as a clean [DIAGRAM/ASSET]. keep every element, label and relationship exactly where I put it. improve only spacing, alignment, line weight and contrast.

the pattern in all thirteen is the same. name what stays before you name what changes, and be boring about it.

every word you leave undefined is a word the model gets to decide for you...
