## 正文

一直在测 ChatGPT Image 2.5，AI UGC 这一跳非常大。现在能从一个 AI 网红生成整库 UGC 广告。15 个用例+提示：

1. 定人设：写实 22 岁某族裔女性，手臂长自拍、略高于眼，卧室窗光，自然皮肤毛孔，无美颜，飞发。9:16。  
2. 锁主图：脸型瞳色鼻唇发际发色发长身体比例肤质永久锁死，以后只改我点名的。  
3. 身份表：图1 为权威参考，4 帧正/左3/4/右3/4/微仰，中性表情同样光。  
4. 定相机：前置手机、手臂长自拍、轻微桶形畸变、室内混白平衡、脸微过曝、淡传感器噪、无棚拍无精修。  
5. 给字留空：9:16，脸在中三分，上三分干净负空间。  
6–10. 反应库：同一女孩同一房间同一手机，只换情绪——震惊、哭、大笑、松口气、死鱼眼「之前」。  
11. 埋诱饵：身份表情光构图锁死，加一个不明显的小细节（T 恤 logo、身后书名、镜子反射），低对比半遮挡，评论区会重看。  
12. 换一天不换人：身份比例锁死，只换服装发型地点（车/床/厨房/浴室镜）。  
13. 选角测试：同一帧 N 个变体，场景构图光表情锁死，只换人（年龄/族裔/头发）。  
14. 真实度：太干净就回炉，只降采集质量：软焦、手微动模糊、更硬窗光、压缩高光。要像发出去的，不像制作出来的。  
15. 再动画：反应帧丢进 Seedance 2.5 当首帧，同一反应提示。Higgsfield 上 2.5 和 Seedance 都能用。

## 原文

been testing ChatGPT Image 2.5 and the jump for AI UGC is massive

you can now generate an entire UGC ad library from one AI influencer

here are 15 use cases + prompts to test:

1) cast her: photorealistic 22 year old [ETHNICITY] woman, front-facing phone selfie, arm’s length, held slightly above eye level. bedroom with soft window light. natural skin texture, visible pores, no makeup filter, flyaway hairs. she looks like a real person filming herself, not a model. 9:16 vertical.

2) lock the master: this is the approved character master. treat face shape, eye color, nose, lips, hairline, hair color and length, body proportions and skin texture as permanently locked. in every future generation, change only what I explicitly name.

3) the identity sheet: using image 1 as the canonical reference, generate the same woman in 4 frames: front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, slight low angle. neutral expression, same lighting. preserve every facial feature exactly. this is my reference set for all future prompts.

4) define the camera: every image from now on uses the same capture spec front-facing phone lens, arm’s length selfie, slight barrel distortion, mixed indoor white balance, mild overexposure on the face, faint sensor noise, no studio lighting, no retouching. amateur but well-lit.

5) leave room for the text: compose all frames 9:16 with her face in the middle third and clean negative space in the upper third.

now the reaction library. same girl, same room, same phone, only the emotion changes:

6) shocked: preserve identity, wardrobe, lighting and framing from the master. expression and pose: eyes wide, eyebrows raised high, jaw genuinely slack, mouth open mid-gasp. one hand halfway to her mouth. caught off guard, not posing.

7) crying: preserve everything from the master. change expression and pose: glassy eyes, one tear on the cheek, red nose and eyelids, chin slightly trembling, brows pulled together. soft emotional crying, not distress. she is still looking at the lens.

8) max joy: preserve everything from the master. change expression and pose: full open-mouth laugh, eyes squeezed almost shut, head tilted back slightly, shoulders raised. genuine unposed laughter with natural asymmetry.

9) relieved sigh: preserve everything from the master. change expression and pose: eyes closed, head tilted back, cheeks puffed exhaling, shoulders dropping. the physical release of tension right after a problem is solved.

10) frustrated (the setup shot): preserve everything from the master. change expression and pose: flat stare into the lens, dead eyes, unimpressed, jaw tight. this is the “before” frame that makes the reaction land.

11) plant the bait: preserve identity, expression, lighting and framing exactly. add ONE small out-of-place detail that isn’t obvious on first watch: a logo on the t-shirt, a title on the book behind her, something in the mirror reflection. keep it low contrast, partially occluded, never the focus of the frame. one person notices, comments about it, and the whole comment section rewatches the ad to find it. that rewatch is free retention.

12) change the day, not the girl: preserve identity and proportions exactly from the master. change ONLY wardrobe, hairstyle and location to [CAR / BED / KITCHEN / BATHROOM MIRROR]. same person, different day, same phone.

13) the roster: generate [N] variants of this exact frame. preserve scene, framing, lighting, camera and expression. change ONLY the person: [age / ethnicity / hair]. same ad, different casting, for testing which face converts.

14) authenticity pass: if a frame looks too clean, run it back: keep every element identical, degrade only the capture, softer focus, slight motion blur on the hand, harsher window light, compressed highlights. it should look posted, not produced.

15) then animate it: drop the reaction frame into seedance 2.5 as the first frame and prompt with the same rection. You can go to Higgsfield to use both seedance 2.5 and gpt images 2.5
