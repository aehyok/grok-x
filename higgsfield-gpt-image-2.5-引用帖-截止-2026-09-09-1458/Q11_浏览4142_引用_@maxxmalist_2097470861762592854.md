## 正文

GPT-Image-2.5 太强了。它让你完整控制图里什么变、什么留。可用提示：

1. 只改一个细节，其他物体纹理颜色比例完全照原样。  
2. UGC 角色帧：同一张脸身体服装，新帧换姿势和场景。  
3. 多轮迭代：每条消息只改我点名的，没提的部分绝不重渲。  
4. 前后对比：生成「之前更差」的状态，除那一处外像素级对齐。  
5. 1:1 改 9:16 不裁切，扩展场景，产品和文字层级仍可读。  
6. 草图变分镜：每个元素位置按我画的来。  
7. 精确改机位：场景主体保留，相机更高/更低。  
8. 透明底产品图：抠出产品修边，产品本身不动。  
9. 室内只改墙/地板/一件家具，建筑窗透视锁死。  
10. 只删一个物体，周围几何阴影纹理可信且不变。  
11. 把这张当上一帧，生成下一镜动作，角色服装地点时间一致。  
12. 清产品：去划痕灰尘，几何品牌材质不动。  
13. 图 2 只当环境参考，把建筑和氛围转到图 1，主体保留。  
14. 只改表情，身份发型服装姿势环境保留。

GPT-Image-2.5 is incredible

it gives you full control over what changes and what stays in an image

here are some prompts you can use:

1. fix one detail: change only [DETAIL]. keep every other object, texture, color and proportion exactly as shown

2. ugc avatar frames: this person is my recurring character. same face, same body, same outfit. new frame: [pose + setting]

3. multi-turn iteration: we will edit this image step by step. each edit changes only what i name in that message. never re-render the parts i didn't mention

4. before/after pairs: generate the 'before' state of this image: [what was worse]. everything except that one element must match the original pixel for pixel

5. format swaps without cropping: rebuild this 1:1 creative as 9:16. extend the scene instead of cropping, keep the product and text hierarchy readable

6. sketch to storyboard: turn my rough sketch into a finished frame in [style]. keep every element exactly where i drew it

7. change the camera position precisely: preserve the scene and subject. recreate the shot from a camera positioned [HIGHER/LOWER] while keeping the visual identity consistent

8. transparent product image: isolate the product on a transparent background. repair edges, change nothing about the product itself

9. interior redesign: redesign only the [wall / floor / furniture piece] in this room. architecture, windows and perspective are locked

10. remove one object: delete only [OBJECT]. keep all surrounding geometry, shadows and textures believable and unchanged

11. continue from the frame: treat this image as the previous frame of a sequence. generate the following shot where [ACTION HAPPENS], keeping characters, wardrobe, location and time consistent

12. clean up the product: preserve the product design exactly. remove scratches, dust and minor imperfections without changing its geometry, branding or materials

13. match the environment: use image 2 as a reference for the environment only. transfer its architectural and atmospheric qualities onto image 1 while preserving the subject

14. change the expression: preserve the subject’s identity, hairstyle, outfit, pose and environment. change only their facial expression from [CURRENT] to [NEW]
