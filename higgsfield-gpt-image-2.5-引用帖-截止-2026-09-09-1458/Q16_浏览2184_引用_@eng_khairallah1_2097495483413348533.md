## 正文

这绝对是金子。

大家都在吹 GPT-Image 2.5 的画质。真正升级他们都错过了：是控制。

你不再整张重抽然后祈祷。锁住画面，点名你要改的那一件，完事。

8 个可复制提示：

1. 同一张脸换年龄：脸特征身份锁死，只改表观年龄。  
2. 只改表情：脸光姿势相同，只换成浅笑/大笑/严肃。  
3. 换天气：场景主体构图精确保留，只改雨/雾/大太阳，光和湿表面匹配。  
4. 换材质：形状几何光锁死，只把某物体材质换成黄铜/哑光/玻璃。  
5. 控人群：主体光构图锁死，只加淡人群或清空背景人。  
6. 改机位高度：主体服装环境精确保留，只换低机位/俯视/平视。  
7. 真照片编辑：当真实照片处理，只做这一处改动，颗粒皮肤纹理自然瑕疵都留，不要磨皮风格化。  
8. 只调色：构图主体细节相同，只套暖电影/冷情绪/褪色胶片调色。

整套技巧：别描述你想要图像「成为」什么。锁住它已经是什么，再点名那一件会动的。这就是全部技能。先收藏。

this is absolutely gold

everyone's hyping GPT-Image 2.5's quality. they're all missing the actual upgrade. it's control.

you don't re-roll the whole image and pray anymore. you lock the frame, name the one thing you want changed, done.

[here are 8 use cases + copy-ready prompts:]

1) age the same face: same person, different decade. identity stays locked.
"keep this exact face, features and identity. change only the apparent age to [age]. preserve everything else."

2) expression tweak: a real smile instead of reshooting the entire portrait.
"keep the face, lighting and pose identical. change ONLY the expression to [soft smile / laughing / serious]. touch nothing else."

3) weather swap: same location, new sky. shadows and reflections follow.
"preserve the scene, subject and composition exactly. change only the weather to [rain / fog / bright sun] and match the light and wet surfaces."

4) material swap: same object, new finish. see it in brass, matte, glass, whatever.
"keep the exact shape, geometry and lighting. change only the material of [object] to [material]. preserve the form."

5) crowd control: add or remove people without disturbing the hero of the shot.
"keep the main subject, lighting and framing locked. change only the background people: [add a light crowd / empty the space]. blend naturally."

6) camera height shift: same scene from a new angle without regenerating it from scratch.
"preserve the subject, wardrobe and environment exactly. change only the camera to [low angle / top-down / eye level]. keep everything consistent."

7) hand it a real photo, keep the realism: edit a genuine photo without it turning plasticky or AI-looking.
"treat this as a real photograph. make only this change: [edit]. preserve the grain, skin texture and natural imperfections. don't smooth or stylize anything."

8) color grade only: change the whole mood with a grade, not a redo.
"keep the composition, subject and detail identical. apply only a [warm cinematic / cold moody / faded film] color grade. change nothing else."

the whole trick: stop describing what you want the image to be. lock what it already is, then name the one thing that moves.

that's the entire skill.
