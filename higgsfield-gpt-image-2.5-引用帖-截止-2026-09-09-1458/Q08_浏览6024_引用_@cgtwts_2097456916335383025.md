## 正文

GPT-Image 2.5 最酷的不只是图更好。

是你可以告诉它改什么，而不把别的搞乱。

我会真用的 12 条：

1. 修小东西：整张不动，把破杯子换成干净的，尺寸角度光影匹配。  
2. 试颜色：同一产品 5 版，只改黑白红蓝黄。  
3. 改氛围：白天街景变雨夜，建筑车人位置不动，只改天气和光。  
4. 改画幅：做成 16:9，不裁主体，两边自然延伸背景。  
5. 产品棚拍：产品不动，放到干净大理石桌、高级棚拍软光。  
6. 清杂物：去掉地板上的箱子和线，家具墙光机位不动。  
7. 换包装：只换瓶子标签为写着 ORIGINAL BLEND 的白标，瓶盖反射透视不动。  
8. 换场景：人衣服姿势不动，场景挪到日出海滩，光影匹配新环境。  
9. 改房间：房间不动，只把沙发换成现代奶油色沙发。  
10. 再拍一镜：主体服装地点作参考，稍低机位再拍一镜。  
11. 抛光草图：草图变干净演示图，标签连接信息位置不变。  
12. 白底产品：去背景放纯白，形状颜色纹理比例不动。

你不再只是让 AI「做一张」，而是告诉它摸哪里、放哪里。Sunburst 和 Flare 在 Higgsfield。

## 原文

the coolest thing about GPT-Image 2.5 isn’t just that it makes better images.

it’s that you can tell it what to change without messing up everything else.

here are 12 prompts i’d actually use:

1. FIX SOMETHING SMALL

“Keep everything in this photo the same, but replace the broken mug with a clean one. Match the size, angle, lighting and shadow.”

2. TRY DIFFERENT COLORS

“Make 5 versions of this product. Keep everything identical and change only the product color to black, white, red, blue and yellow.”

3. CHANGE THE VIBE

“Turn this daytime street photo into a rainy evening scene. Keep the buildings, cars and people exactly where they are. Only change the weather and lighting.”

4. CHANGE THE FORMAT

“Turn this image into a 16:9 landscape image. Don’t crop the subject. Extend the background naturally on both sides.”

5. MAKE A PRODUCT SHOT

“Keep the product exactly as shown. Put it on a clean marble table in a premium studio setting with soft lighting and realistic shadows.”

6. CLEAN UP THE MESS

“Remove the boxes and cables from the floor. Fill in the missing areas naturally. Leave the furniture, walls, lighting and camera angle unchanged.”

7. CHANGE THE PACKAGING

“Replace only the label on this bottle with a simple white label that says ‘ORIGINAL BLEND’. Keep the bottle, cap, reflections and perspective exactly the same.”

8. MOVE THE SUBJECT

“Keep the person, clothes and pose unchanged. Move the scene to a quiet beach at sunrise. Make the lighting and shadows match the new environment.”

9. REDESIGN A ROOM

“Keep the room exactly the same. Replace only the sofa with a modern cream-colored sofa. Match the original perspective, lighting and proportions.”

10. MAKE ANOTHER SHOT

“Use this image as the reference for the subject, outfit and location. Create another shot from a slightly lower camera angle while keeping everything else consistent.”

11. POLISH A SKETCH

“Turn this rough sketch into a clean presentation graphic. Keep every label, connection and piece of information exactly where it belongs. Improve only the layout and visual design.”

12. CLEAN UP A PRODUCT PHOTO

“Remove the background and place the product on pure white. Do not change its shape, color, texture or proportions.”

this is where image editing starts getting really interesting.

you’re not just asking AI to make something anymore.

you’re telling it exactly what to touch and what to leave alone.
