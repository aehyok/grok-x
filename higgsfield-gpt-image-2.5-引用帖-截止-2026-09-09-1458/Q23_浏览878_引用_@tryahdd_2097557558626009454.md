## 正文

封面才是 YouTube 的整盘棋，多数无脸创作者还在跟它打架。

要么文字渲不干净，要么 AI 给你六根手指和融化的脸，要么 Canva 一小时还是很一般。

我在 Higgsfield 上用 GPT-Image 2.5 测封面，单是文字渲染就已经改写这件事。

如果今天开无脸频道我会跑的工作流：

- 先写标题，再围着标题做封面，不是反过来。图为点击服务，不为你的自我。  
- 描述情绪，不描述场景。「震惊的脸对着发光屏幕，暗背景，一个亮焦点」永远打过「一个人在电脑前」。  
- 把字烤进图里。以前 AI 封面字永远乱码要手改，这部分现在真修好了。干净可读，最多 3–4 个词。  
- 一个主体、一种情绪、高对比。手机上邮票大小看不懂，细节再多也死。  
- 同一想法出 5–6 版，让 CTR 选赢家。别跟第一张结婚。

封面决定你做的别的东西有没有人看见。它该比脚本更值钱，多数人却给它最少时间。

## 原文

thumbnails are the whole game on youtube and most faceless creators are still fighting with them

you either can't get text to render clean, or the AI gives you six fingers and a face that looks melted, or you spend an hour in canva and it still looks mid

been testing GPT-Image 2.5 on higgsfield for thumbnails and the text rendering alone is the thing that changes it

here's the actual workflow i'd run if i was starting a faceless channel today:

⁃ write your title FIRST, then build the thumbnail around it not the other way around. the image serves the click, not your ego.
⁃ describe the emotion, not the scene. "shocked face reacting to a glowing screen, dark background, one bright focal point" beats "a man at a computer" every time.
⁃ get the text baked into the image itself. this used to be the impossible part AI thumbnails always came out with garbled nonsense text you had to redo by hand. that's the part that's actually fixed now. clean, readable, 3-4 words max.
⁃ one subject, one emotion, high contrast. if you can't understand it at postage-stamp size on a phone, it's dead on arrival no matter how detailed it is.
⁃ generate 5-6 versions of the same idea and let the CTR pick the winner. never marry your first one.

the thumbnail decides whether anything else you made even gets seen. it's worth more time than the script and most people give it the least.
