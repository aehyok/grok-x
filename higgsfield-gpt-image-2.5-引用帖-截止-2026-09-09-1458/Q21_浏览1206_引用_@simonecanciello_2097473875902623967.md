## 正文

现在开始用的人，App 会 10 倍提升。

gpt-image 2.5 终于解决了 AI 修图最大的问题之一：改一件事却把别的全毁。这对移动 App 非常有用。

我会拿来：

1. 同一条赢广告测 20 个钩子：只把标题换成 [NEW HOOK]，人、App 屏、背景、颜色、光、构图和其他元素完全一样。  
2. 测不同商店价值主张：只换主标题为 [NEW VALUE PROP]，设备、App 屏、背景、字体、间距、布局不变。  
3. 几秒本地化：只把可见文字译成西语，字体风格、大小、位置、颜色、间距、视觉层级原样。  
4. 把 App 屏换进爆款格式：只换手机上显示的内容为提供的 App 屏，手、手机角度、反射、光、环境完全一样。  
5. 一条 UGC 变 20 版：创作者、姿势、衣服、手机、机位、光相同，只把背景换成休闲现代卧室。  
6. 测完全不同受众：概念、构图、姿势、手机位置、光相同，只把人换成普通大学年龄女性创作者。  
7. 精修广告变自然手机照：内容元素不动，只让图看起来像自然智能手机照片，随意光、略不完美构图、真实相机处理。  
8. 测不同付费墙报价：只把价格和优惠文案改成「7 天免费」，付费墙设计、字体、按钮、颜色、布局完全一样。  
9. 赢素材做季节版：整条创意相同，只把环境换成微妙冬日设定，不改主体、App、构图或光风格。  
10. 任意迭代：只把 [ELEMENT] 改成 [NEW VERSION]。不要修改、重设计、重新解释或重生成图的任何其他部分。

这意味着：1 条赢创意 × 10 钩子 × 5 受众 × 5 国家 × 3 报价 = 同一概念下 750 次测试。收藏。下次找到能跑的创意会用到这些提示。Sunburst 和 Flare 在 Higgsfield。

whoever starts using this now will 10x improve their app.

gpt-image 2.5 finally solved one of the biggest problems with ai image editing:

changing ONE thing without destroying everything else.

this is insanely useful for mobile apps.

here’s what i’d use it for:

1. test 20 hooks on the same winning ad

“CHANGE ONLY THE HEADLINE TO ‘[NEW HOOK]’. KEEP THE PERSON, APP SCREEN, BACKGROUND, COLORS, LIGHTING, COMPOSITION AND EVERY OTHER ELEMENT EXACTLY THE SAME.”

2. test different app store value props

“REPLACE ONLY THE MAIN HEADLINE WITH ‘[NEW VALUE PROP]’. KEEP THE DEVICE, APP SCREEN, BACKGROUND, TYPOGRAPHY, SPACING AND LAYOUT UNCHANGED.”

3. localize your best creatives in seconds

“TRANSLATE ONLY THE VISIBLE TEXT INTO SPANISH. PRESERVE THE EXACT FONT STYLE, SIZE, POSITION, COLORS, SPACING AND VISUAL HIERARCHY.”

4. swap your app into viral creative formats

“REPLACE ONLY THE CONTENT DISPLAYED ON THE PHONE WITH THE PROVIDED APP SCREEN. KEEP THE HAND, PHONE ANGLE, REFLECTIONS, LIGHTING AND ENVIRONMENT EXACTLY THE SAME.”

5. turn one ugc creative into 20 versions

“KEEP THE CREATOR, POSE, CLOTHES, PHONE, CAMERA ANGLE AND LIGHTING IDENTICAL. CHANGE ONLY THE BACKGROUND TO A CASUAL MODERN BEDROOM.”

6. test completely different audiences

“KEEP THE EXACT CONCEPT, COMPOSITION, POSE, PHONE POSITION AND LIGHTING. REPLACE ONLY THE PERSON WITH A GENERIC COLLEGE-AGE FEMALE CREATOR.”

7. make polished ads look organic

“KEEP ALL CONTENT AND ELEMENTS UNCHANGED. ONLY MAKE THE IMAGE FEEL LIKE A NATURAL SMARTPHONE PHOTO WITH CASUAL LIGHTING, SLIGHTLY IMPERFECT FRAMING AND REALISTIC CAMERA PROCESSING.”

8. test different paywall offers

“CHANGE ONLY THE PRICE AND OFFER TEXT TO ‘7 DAYS FREE’. KEEP THE PAYWALL DESIGN, TYPOGRAPHY, BUTTONS, COLORS AND LAYOUT EXACTLY THE SAME.”

9. create seasonal versions of winners

“KEEP THE ENTIRE CREATIVE IDENTICAL. CHANGE ONLY THE ENVIRONMENT TO A SUBTLE WINTER SETTING WITHOUT ALTERING THE SUBJECT, APP, COMPOSITION OR LIGHTING STYLE.”

10. iterate literally anything

“CHANGE ONLY [ELEMENT] TO [NEW VERSION]. DO NOT MODIFY, REDESIGN, REINTERPRET OR REGENERATE ANY OTHER PART OF THE IMAGE.”

this means:

1 winning creative
10 hooks
5 audiences
5 countries
3 offers

= 750 tests from the same concept.

save this post.

you’ll need these prompts the next time you find a creative that works.
