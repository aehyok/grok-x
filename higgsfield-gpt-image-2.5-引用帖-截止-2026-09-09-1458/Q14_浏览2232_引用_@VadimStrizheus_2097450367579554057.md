## 正文

一小处编辑不该重画整张图。15 条实测 GPT-Image 2.5 控制力的提示：

1. 拼参考：图1管成人外貌，图2管素色衣服，图3管房间，合成一张自然照片，光要统一。  
2. 保护已批准图：只把靠垫从奶油改橄榄绿，沙发形状面料房间机位光都留着。  
3. 去掉一根线：删桌边松线，用周围纹理补地板那一小块，桌椅阴影其余不动。  
4. 产品图变广告：无品牌陶瓷杯放到浅石台窗边，把手釉面比例精确，阴影匹配窗光，右侧留文案空。  
5. 测一个变量：米色背景改淡蓝，产品位置尺寸标题字体光裁切都一样。  
6. 方形扩 9:16：上下延伸场景，主体完整、原比例，上四分之一别挤。  
7. UI 进发布视觉：原仪表盘截图放到笔记本屏幕，每个标签图表忠实，只为贴屏改透视。  
8. 印到布上：几何图案按褶皱缝线贴到帆布袋，颜色形状保留，提手尺寸背景不动。  
9. 分镜续：原角色和工房作参考，下一镜更近伸手拿木盒，外观衣服盒子房间光一致。  
10. 建模参考：凳子正面浅灰，腿间距座厚比例材质保留，均匀光少透视。  
11. 换装：用图2亚麻夹克换主体夹克，脸头发姿势裤子保留。  
12. 室内预览：只把橱柜门改哑光鼠尾草绿，把手台面电器地板窗机位保留。  
13. 改文案：SPRING COLLECTION 换成 SUMMER COLLECTION，字体字重对齐匹配。  
14. 清草图：流程草图变白底干净图，措辞箭头方向原样，间距线更清晰，不加步骤。  
15. 目录图：无品牌玻璃花瓶抠白底，轮廓色泽透明度保留，柔接触阴影，原裁切比例。

## 原文

one small edit shouldn’t redraw your whole image.

here are 15 practical prompts to test GPT-Image 2.5’s control over what changes and what stays:

1. combine references:

 “Use image 1 for the adult subject’s appearance, image 2 for the plain outfit, and image 3 for the room. Build one natural photograph. Keep each reference limited to its assigned role and match the lighting across the scene.”

2. protect an approved image:

 “Use this image as the baseline. Change only the cushion from cream to olive green. Keep the sofa’s shape, fabric texture, room layout, camera angle and lighting as they are.”

3. fix one distracting detail:

 “Remove the loose cable beside the desk. Reconstruct the small area of floor it covers using the surrounding texture. Leave the desk, chair, shadows and rest of the photograph unchanged.”

4. turn a product shot into an ad:

 “Place this unbranded ceramic mug on a pale stone kitchen counter beside a window. Preserve its exact handle, rim, glaze and proportions. Match its shadow to the window light and leave clear space on the right for copy.”

5. test one creative variable:

 “Make a version of this image with a pale blue background instead of beige. Keep the product in the same position and size. Preserve the headline, typography, lighting and crop so the background color is the only test variable.”

6. make a vertical version:

“Expand this square image into a 9:16 composition by extending the scene above and below. Keep the entire subject visible at its original proportions. Continue the existing background naturally and leave the upper quarter uncluttered.”

7. put a UI into a launch visual:

“Place this original, unbranded dashboard screenshot on the screen of a generic laptop on a dark desk. Keep every visible label and chart faithful to the upload. Adjust only the perspective needed to fit the screen; keep it readable.”

8. preview a print on fabric:

“Apply this original geometric pattern to the plain tote bag in image 2. Follow the fabric’s folds and seams. Preserve the pattern’s colors and shapes, and keep the bag’s handles, dimensions and background unchanged.”

9. continue a storyboard:

 “Use this original adult character and workshop as the reference for the next frame. Show a closer view of the character reaching for the wooden box. Keep their appearance, clothing, the box design and the room’s lighting consistent.”

10. create a modeling reference:

“Show this original stool directly from the front against light gray. Preserve its leg spacing, seat thickness, proportions and material. Use even lighting and minimal perspective distortion so its construction is easy to read.”

11. swap an outfit:

 “Replace the adult subject’s jacket with the plain linen jacket in image 2. Preserve the subject’s face, hair, pose and trousers. Fit the jacket naturally around the existing body position and match the original lighting.”

12. preview an interior change:

 “Change only the cabinet fronts in this kitchen to matte sage green. Preserve the handles, countertop, appliances, floor, windows and camera position. Retain the existing surface texture and shadows.”

13. update text in a creative:

 “Replace ‘SPRING COLLECTION’ with ‘SUMMER COLLECTION’. Match the existing type style, weight and alignment. Fit the new wording within the same text area and preserve every other part of the image.”

14. clean up a diagram:

 “Turn this original process sketch into a clean diagram on white. Keep the wording and arrow directions exactly as drawn. Improve spacing and line clarity while preserving every connection. Add no extra steps.”

15. prepare a catalog image:

 “Isolate this unbranded glass vase on a white background. Preserve its outline, tint and transparency. Remove the surrounding objects, retain a soft contact shadow, and keep the original crop and product scale.”
