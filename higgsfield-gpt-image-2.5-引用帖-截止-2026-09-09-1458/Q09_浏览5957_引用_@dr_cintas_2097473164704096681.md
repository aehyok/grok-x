## 正文

GPT-Image 2.5 的控制力好得吓人。12 个可偷的用例：

1. 冻住主体换场景：人姿势构图不动，只换背景到新地点，主体上的光仍匹配原图。  
2. 一字产品测试：4 版广告只换标题，布局产品图颜色相同。  
3. 修瑕疵不修照片：去掉脸左侧阴影，肤色头发背景都别动。  
4. 改尺寸不重设计：扩展到新比例，不拉伸不裁主体，补匹配的新背景。  
5. 同一模特换装：参考模特姿势布光，换上新服装，身体脸机位不移。  
6. 包装必须精确：标签设计已定不能改，按真实包裹阴影材质贴到产品形状上。  
7. 序列下一帧：这是第 1 帧，生成第 2 帧，角色场景画风相同只改动作。  
8. 三视图：用角色参考生成背面同姿势光比例，给 3D 参考表。  
9. 换季节不换镜头：同一建筑机位时刻，只换冬/夏，不改建筑。  
10. 翻译文字：把牌子上的字换成指定译文，角度光背景不变。  
11. 改房间一个特征：真房间布局，只改地板/墙色/家具风格，墙窗机位原地。  
12. 上架白底：产品隔离到纯白，形状颜色比例不动。

## 原文

GPT-Image 2.5’s control is scary good.

12 use cases + prompts to steal:

1. freeze the subject, swap the scene: "keep the person, pose, and framing exactly as-is. only change the background to [NEW LOCATION]. lighting on the subject should still match the original shot."

2. one-word product tests: "generate 4 versions of this ad. the only difference between them is the headline: [A] / [B] / [C] / [D]. layout, product shot, and colors must stay identical."

3. fix a flaw, not the photo: "there's a shadow on the left side of the subject's face. remove just that. don't touch anything else, including skin tone, hair, or background."

4. resize without redesigning: "extend this image to [ASPECT RATIO]. don't stretch or crop the subject, add new background content that matches the existing scene so the composition still feels intentional."

5. same model, new outfit: "this is the reference model, pose, and lighting setup. put them in [OUTFIT] instead. body, face, and camera angle should not shift."

6. packaging that has to stay exact: "this label design is final and cannot change. show it applied to [PRODUCT SHAPE] with realistic wrap, shadow, and material behavior."

7. next frame in a sequence: "this is frame 1 of a story. generate frame 2: [WHAT HAPPENS NEXT]. same characters, same setting, same art style, only the action changes."

8. turnaround sheet: "using this character as reference, generate a back view in the same pose, lighting, and proportions. this is for a 3D reference sheet, so consistency matters more than creativity."

9. swap the season, not the shot: "same building, same camera angle, same time of day. change the season to [WINTER/SUMMER/etc]. don't alter the architecture.”

10. translate any text: "replace the text on the sign with '[TRANSLATED TEXT]' in [LANGUAGE]. everything else in the image, angle, lighting, background, stays the same."

11. redesign one room feature: "this is a real room layout. change only the [FLOORING/WALL COLOR/FURNITURE STYLE]. walls, windows, and camera position must stay in the exact same place."

12. clean product shot for a listing: "isolate the product on a plain white background. don't change its shape, color, or proportions, just remove the environment around it."
