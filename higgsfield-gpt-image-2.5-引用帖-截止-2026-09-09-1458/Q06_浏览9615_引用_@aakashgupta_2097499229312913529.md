## 正文

AI 图像真正难的从来不是生成。是让东西保持不变。

以前每个模型，改一次等于整张重抽。让 logo 小一点，脸漂了、光变了、产品歪了。你拿 95% 喜欢的部分去赌那 5%。

GPT-Image 2.5 是第一个：我告诉它什么冻结，它就遵守。这才解锁真正的生产。12 个可直接粘贴的用例：

1. 锁定角色：把这张图当永久参考，以后每次编辑，脸、身体、头发、服装除非我点名否则完全一样。  
2. 同一产品换场景：产品形状材质颜色 logo 锁死，只换环境、光、机位。  
3. 只改一个区域：只编辑 [REGION]，周围光、纹理、透视匹配，外面不动。  
4. 广告 A/B：4 个版本，布局产品和文字相同，只变背景色。  
5. 改比例不裁切：重组成 9:16 竖图，不裁，扩展场景并重排，主体仍占主导。  
6. 截图放进宣传片：UI 一个像素都不改，放到笔记本屏幕上，真实反射。  
7. 图案印到周边：把这幅图精确印到马克杯上，顺着曲面，颜色保真。  
8. 换装：图 1 的人、姿势、光、背景，穿上图 2 的夹克，剪裁面料缝线保留。  
9. 房间改造：墙窗门机位锁死，家具换成胡桃木+奶油色中世纪。  
10. 设计本地化：布局图像光线锁死，只换标题文字，按市场微调背景。  
11. 修瑕疵留照片：去掉 [FLAW]，按颗粒、对焦、光线重建后面，看不出修过。  
12. 草图变完成图：白板草图重建成干净信息图，每个框箭头标签位置不变，只改间距字体抛光。

## 原文

The hard problem in AI images was never generation. It was keeping things the same.

Every model until now ran each edit as a full reroll. Ask for a smaller logo and the face drifted, the lighting shifted, the product warped. You gambled the 95% you loved to fix the 5% you didn't.

GPT-Image 2.5 is the first one where I tell it what's frozen and it complies. That unlocks actual production work. 12 use cases with prompts you can paste straight in.

1. Lock a character
"treat this image as the permanent reference for this character. in every future edit, keep face, body, hair and outfit identical unless I name the change."

2. Same product, new scene
"this product is the fixed reference. keep its shape, materials, colors and logo exact. place it in a rooftop bar at dusk and change only the setting, light and camera."

3. Edit one region only
"edit only the [REGION]. match the surrounding lighting, texture and perspective. leave everything outside it untouched."

4. Ad variants for testing
"make 4 versions of this creative. keep the layout, product and text identical. vary only the background color."

5. Resize without cropping
"recompose this image as a 9:16 vertical. do not crop. extend the scene and rearrange elements so the subject stays dominant."

6. Screenshot into a campaign shot
"this UI is finished artwork. do not change a pixel of it. show it on a laptop screen in a bright studio scene with realistic reflections."

7. Artwork onto merch
"apply this exact artwork to a ceramic mug. follow the curve of the surface and how print sits on it. keep every color true."

8. Outfit swap
"keep the person, pose, lighting and background from image 1. dress them in the jacket from image 2, preserving its cut, fabric and stitching."

9. Room makeover
"keep this room's walls, windows, doors and camera angle exactly. swap the furniture for mid-century pieces in walnut and cream."

10. Localize a design
"keep this design's layout, imagery and lighting. replace the headline with '[NEW TEXT]' and adjust small background details for [MARKET]. add nothing else."

11. Fix the flaw, spare the photo
"remove the [FLAW] from this shot. rebuild what sits behind it to match grain, focus and light. nobody should be able to tell anything was removed."

12. Sketch to finished graphic
"rebuild this whiteboard sketch as a clean infographic. every box, arrow and label stays where it is. improve only spacing, type and polish."
