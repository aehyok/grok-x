Why does a 'Claw' tool spin out of control?
It comes down to two words: NO BOUNDARIES.
The prevailing design philosophy for Claw-like tools is to grant maximal 'permissions.' Let the AI be as free and uninhibited as possible. The more powerful, the better.
The result is a product that is:
Blind to 'off-limits' zones.
Acts before asking you for permission.
Spends all your tokens on nonsense.
Forgets your specific rules instantly.
The more powerful the tool, the more dangerous it becomes.
This isn't your fault as a user; it's a fundamental failure in product design.
We chose the 'counter-intuitive' path.

What's AES-256? It's considered uncrackable by modern standards.
