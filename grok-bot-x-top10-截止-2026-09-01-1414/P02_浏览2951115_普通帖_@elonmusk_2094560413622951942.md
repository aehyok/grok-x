原文：

A few weeks after our newborns arrived, I kept worrying about getting their bath water temperature right, since newborn skin is so much more sensitive.

So I used it as an opportunity to test Grok 4.6 and built a tiny Apple Watch app that checks the water temperature and tells me when it’s too hot or cold.

Grok Bot was also surprisingly useful for shipping it: it navigated the App Store submission process through the browser, helped fill out the forms, and even helped answer Apple’s questions during review.

It’s now on the App Store. If anyone wants to try it, I’d love feedback.
