## 正文

作者两天烧掉一周 Grok Bot 用量的约 70%。学到的是：怎么叫醒 Agent，比把聊天整理得好看更决定账单。

Grok Bot 不是按消息计费，而是按 agent 步骤和 token。每周重置，Mac 和 iOS 共用一池。

最差情况是开全员频道。别人不需要做事就别打开。一条消息会叫醒所有人。五个 Agent 在房间里，等于五轮、五串步骤同时跑。1:1 发给负责那个任务的 Agent，通常就够。

别让全组争论怎么干活，那也烧用量。一个人负责、一份交付物就够。记录放到共享板或日志，不要在频道里再广播「我已经记过了」。

如果真正收口的是专家，直接跟专家说。经过协调者等于同一张票走两轮：协调者只路由、不干活。

一直迭代却不交付的 Agent 也吃用量。砍循环，别砍看板。

定时别太勤。每 15 分钟跑一次，一天次数很多，每次都花 token。小时级或一天几次通常够。长聊天会让这些运行更贵。周期性任务交给新 Bot，长线程留给主 Bot / 协调者。

落地：

- 活发在 1:1
- 频道留给全队必须听到的事
- 长活之前先看套餐用量页
- 从小范围开始
- 额度花在 Agent 做了什么，不花在聊天看起来多整齐

第一天的建议：别一上来就开满员频道。一个职责清楚的 Agent 往往够。

## 原文

I almost burned through my weekly Grok Bot usage in two days. Here's what I learned.

First, what Grok Bot is. It's an xAI product that builds AI teammates. Not a chat that answers and dies. You can spin up a team of agents. Each one can have a name, a defined job, its own conversation and memory. They get into your apps through plugins or a browser. They keep files on a cloud computer that doesn't vanish when you close the laptop. You can give them skills, reusable ways to do the work, and routines that run those skills on a schedule or when something happens. If they need to publish, pay, or delete, they ask for approval.

Each agent is responsible for a task. You give them the task and they run it.
I used it to set up flows I didn't have. Content, learning, social for a company. Pretty much anything you want to automate. I got excited and created a bunch right away, customized them, wrote their jobs. I'm just getting started and the setup already helped a lot.
A tip from day one is don't start with a channel full of agents like I did. One agent with a clear job is often enough.
The problem was the pace. In two days I was already near 70% of my weekly usage. I saw it on my account. I went looking at how they bill and how not to burn it so fast.
Grok Bot doesn't charge you per message. It charges for agent steps and tokens. Usage resets every week and Mac and iOS pull from the same pool. What you learn along the way is how much it matters how you wake the agents.
The channel is the worst case. If the others don't need to do anything, don't open it. One message there wakes everyone. Five agents in the room means five turns and five streaks of steps at once. A 1:1 to the agent responsible for that task wakes one. That's usually enough.
Another tip is don't let the group argue about the work. That burns usage too. One person responsible for that task and a deliverable is enough. The rest can read. If you need a record, put it on a shared board or log. The record is useful. Telling the whole room you already logged it, not so much.
If the specialist is the one who closes the work, talk to them. Going through the coordinator is two turns for the same ticket, because the coordinator routes and doesn't do the job.
An agent that keeps iterating and never delivers also eats usage. Cut the loop, not the board.
Another tip is don't set routines too often. One every 15 minutes runs a lot of times a day, and each run spends tokens. Hourly, or a few times a day, is usually enough. A long chat with the bot also makes those runs more expensive. If the routine is recurring, give it to a fresh bot and keep the long thread with your main bots, the coordinator for example.
What I landed on.
• Send the work in 1:1.
• Save the channel for what the whole team needs to hear.
• Before a long job, check usage on the plan screen.
• Start with a small scope.
• Credit gets spent on what the agent does, not on how tidy the chat looks.
