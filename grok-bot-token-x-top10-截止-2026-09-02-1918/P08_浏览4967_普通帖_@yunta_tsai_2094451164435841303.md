## 正文

Grok Bot 在这个演示里并不怎么烧 token。

它不需要。多数时候它把活交给 Grok Imagine 和语音，自己只负责转向。

多数人的 token 问题，来自定时任务每隔几分钟就把超长聊天记录再送一遍（routine 循环）。

作者的演示是干净的群聊，没有长聊天史。

## 原文

Grok @bot does not burn many tokens on this.

It doesn’t need to. Most of the time it hands off to Grok @imagine and voice. All they need to do is steer.

Most people’s token problems are due to a routine that repeatedly sends a long context of chat history (like routine loops) every few minutes.

In my demonstration, it’s a clean group chat. There’s no long chat history.
