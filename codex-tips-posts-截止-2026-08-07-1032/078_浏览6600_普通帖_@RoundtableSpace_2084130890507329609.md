## 正文

Simple Codex tip worth saving.

Tell Sol to configure Luna Max as a subagent by creating a custom agent at ~/.codex/agents/luna-worker.toml. Set the model to gpt-5.6-luna, reasoning effort to max, give it a description and instructions for bounded delegated work, then tell it to validate against your installed Codex version and show you the diff before using luna_worker for subagent tasks.

Sol orchestrates. Luna handles the routine work. You stop burning Sol tokens on tasks that don't need them.
