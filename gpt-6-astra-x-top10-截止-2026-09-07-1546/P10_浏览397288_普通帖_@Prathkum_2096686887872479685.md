## 原文

I have a genuine question.

Anthropic has probably been working on Fable 5.1 for a very long time. OpenAI has probably been working on GPT-6 Astra for just as long. These labs are almost certainly building in parallel, not reacting to each other in real time of course.

So why does Fable 5.1 launch just a couple of days before Astra, and then Astra beats it on almost every benchmark by a good margin?

And this isn't a one-off.

Every single launch follows the same pattern: when Anthropic ships a new model, it beats all of OpenAI's previous models.
When OpenAI ships a new model, it beats all of Anthropic's previous models. Now they are actually beating what the other just launched days earlier.

Benchmarks are completely vanity metrics.
