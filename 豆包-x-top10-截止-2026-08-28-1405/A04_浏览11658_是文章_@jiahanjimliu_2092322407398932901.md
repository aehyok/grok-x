## 正文

The closest estimate to how many tokens are served globally is https://tokensperday.com/. This includes both open and closed source models. The methodology uses an floor of verified token counts then for companies which do not disclose live token production like Anthropic and OpenAI, they use last known datapoints to make projections using energy usage, hardware capacity, revenue. For example,  OpenAI had last disclosed 15b tokens per minute served on April 8, 2026 (1).

Periods of Flatness
We see there there are periods of flatness where tokens per day level out before continuing on a large ramp again. This is likely because when a new generation of models roll out, each token is more intelligent but consumes more compute. As a result less tokens are produced per GPU/ASIC even more compute is used. However overall expanding compute capacity keeps the curve non-negative besides a period in Feb 2026 when Opus 4.6 released and Opus 4.6 tokens displaced cheaper tokens faster than compute could expand.

At the global floor curve we are seeing a semi-leveling due to the fact that now open source has moved to quality tokens > numerous cheaper tokens. This is direct evidence of Jevons at the model level - all compute will be used for higher intelligence. Kimi K3 is already approaching the intelligence density per token and cost of OpenAI/Anthropic (2).

A corollary is that Anthropic no longer has the intelligence advantage it did on OpenAI or and gap is relatively smaller to open source.

Economic Value
 What's important to note is that producing alot of tokens alone does not correlate to profits.

@FireworksAI_HQ is the leading Open Source AI Platform and produces a whooping 30T tokens per day which is more than both Anthropic at 25.8T and OpenAI at 21.6T but cannot compare in terms of profits. This is because FireworksAI is the SaaS layer that customers buy on top of bare metal from  Hyperscalers, $IREN, $CRWV.

What this implies is that for Open Source, the economic profits go to Nvidia or the bare metal GPUs + Nvidia foundational software.

Major Players
Many people haven't heard of Doubao, the number one producer of tokens in the world. ByteDance, the parent company of TikTok, also owns Doubao. Doubao produces tokens for Doubao app, TikTok, Douyin and much of China's social media and shopping tokens. Doubao has it's own AI Cloud for  China. You can see where Meta Cloud got it's inspiration from.

Likewise Google is the largest token producer in the US and entire West despite all the attention to OpenAI / Anthropic. This is because Google's whole product line has moved onto Gemini. Google does not need the best model, it just needs a good enough model to not pay the OpenAI / Anthropic tax. In fact, Meta was buying Gemini tokens for a long time before it got limited (2) and is migrating to it's in-house Muse models.

Real ROI
It's clear that the majority of tokens by volume is used in the social media / advertisement / shopping power houses today of ByteDance, Google, Meta. I suspect Meta has very high token production but isn't in the chart since they don't have figures to project from. These are huge cash flowing machines whose leaders are very tech savvy hence they are the first to adopt AI. This is not at all pets-dot-com because AI is the foundation to these trillion dollar businesses right now. Failure to adopt AI means loosing market share.

Premium Tokens
So the question becomes why is all the focus around OpenAI and Anthropic if ByteDance and Google produce so many more tokens? The answers is because the profit lies mostly in premium intelligence, being on the frontier of intelligence derives all the profits. Most of the rest of the profits goes to Nvidia which is the common denominator cost whether you use OpenAI tokens or Kimi K3 tokens on Modal AI Platform.
